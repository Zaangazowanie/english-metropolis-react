Types of cookies

Within our websites we use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential/Technical: These files are necessary for the proper functioning of the website. They are installed in particular to remember login sessions or to fill in forms, and to remember whether you have been informed about the use of cookies.
  • Functional: They remember and adapt the platform to your choices, e.g. they allow the e-mail address last used to log in to the platform to be filled in automatically.
  • Analytical: They make it possible to check the number of visits and traffic sources. With their help we can determine which pages are popular and which are not, examine statistics and improve the performance of our sites. The EnglishMetro service does not currently use analytical or marketing tools — if they are introduced, this policy will be updated and the user's consent will be obtained before they are enabled.
  • Marketing: They allow displayed advertising content to be matched to the user's interests, not only within our portal but also outside it. They may be installed by advertising partners. Based on information from these cookies and activity on other websites, a profile of the user's interests is built.
  • Social media cookies: These are cookies installed by our partners to match displayed advertising content in social media. A profile of the user's interests is built on their basis. As a result, displayed content is matched to the user's individual needs.

Cookie storage period

  • Session - stored on the device used until the site is left, e.g. by closing the web browser.
  • Persistent - exist until they are manually deleted or until the time specified in the browser expires.

Type of data collected

This includes:

  • the IP address of the device used by the user;
  • the device type;
  • time spent on the site;
  • actions taken on the site;
  • the location from which the connection is made.

How to withdraw consent

Some cookies are downloaded by the web browser when the website is first opened. To block the downloading of cookies, use the method available for your browser:

  • Firefox
  • Chrome
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Safari

The inability to save/read cookies may result in incomplete and incorrect functioning of the website, but using the site will still be possible.
