01

Who provides the service

The englishmetro.com platform is provided by Michael Poncana, trading as English Metropolis ("English Metropolis", "we", "us").

Contact for all matters relating to the platform and these terms: support@englishmetro.com.

02

What the service is

englishmetro.com is an online learning platform that accompanies English lessons taught by English Metropolis teachers. It provides:

  • personal lesson summaries, feedback and progress tracking;
  • personalised vocabulary lists with examples, pronunciation and practice activities;
  • quizzes, games and an AI conversation tutor;
  • for partner schools: an administration area for managing groups, scheduling and lesson records.

Access to the platform is included in your English course. The platform itself does not charge separate fees.

03

Student accounts

  • Accounts are created by English Metropolis for enrolled students. You cannot self-register.
  • Keep your login credentials confidential. Your personal learning page contains your private learning data.
  • Tell us immediately at support@englishmetro.com if you believe someone else has accessed your account.
  • Accounts are personal and may not be shared with or transferred to other people.
04

Acceptable use

When using the platform you must not:

  • attempt to access other students' data or any part of the platform you are not authorised to use;
  • copy, scrape or mass-download platform content;
  • use the AI tutor to generate unlawful, offensive or harmful content;
  • interfere with the security or operation of the platform;
  • use the platform for any purpose other than personal English learning (or school administration, for authorised staff).

We may suspend accounts that violate these rules. Where reasonable, we will warn you first.

05

Learning materials and copyright

All learning materials on the platform — lesson summaries, vocabulary sets, exercises, games and design — are the intellectual property of English Metropolis or used under licence.

You may use them for your personal study only. You may not republish, sell or distribute them.

Embedded pronunciation videos are provided by YouTube and remain the property of their respective owners.

06

Lesson transcripts and AI feedback

To prepare your personalised feedback, lessons may be transcribed and analysed, including with the help of artificial intelligence. This is described in detail in our Privacy Policy, including your right to object.

AI-generated feedback is a learning aid. While we review it, language assessments are estimates and may contain errors — they are not official language certificates.

07

Lessons, scheduling and cancellations

Lesson prices, schedules and payment terms are agreed separately as part of your course agreement (individually or through your school/employer).

Unless your course agreement says otherwise, lessons cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged in full. Lessons cancelled with more than 24 hours' notice are not charged.

Availability of the platform

We work hard to keep the platform available at all times, but we cannot guarantee uninterrupted access. The platform may occasionally be unavailable due to maintenance, updates or circumstances beyond our control.

The platform is an accompaniment to your lessons — temporary unavailability of the platform does not affect lessons themselves.

Liability

We are liable for damage caused by our intentional misconduct or gross negligence. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for:

  • temporary unavailability of the platform;

  • decisions you make based on AI-generated language assessments.

Nothing in these terms limits your statutory rights as a consumer under Polish and EU consumer protection law.

Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by Polish law. Any disputes will be resolved by the competent Polish courts, without prejudice to mandatory consumer protections that let you bring proceedings in your own country of residence.

We may update these terms from time to time. Significant changes will be announced to active students by email or on the platform at least 14 days before they take effect. If you do not accept the new terms, you may stop using the platform and end your course according to your course agreement.
