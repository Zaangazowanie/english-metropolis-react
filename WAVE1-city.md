# Wave 1 — city lane (branch `play/city`, worktree `/root/em-wt-city`)

Owner brief answered here: the built environment. Buildings with real depth, streets with kerbs and
furniture, vegetation with species, a landmark per district, a hub that reads as a station
forecourt, and streaming that no longer hitches or leaks. Characters, lighting/post and the
gameplay system belong to the other two lanes.

## What the player now sees

- **Districts are generated by a kit, not boxes.** `src/kit/facades.js` builds every wall as an
  extruded slab with true openings; each opening gets a recessed pane 0.2 m in, a frame, a sill
  and a head (flat lintel, brick arch with keystone, sash cross); doors get fanlights, steps,
  porticos or hoods; shopfronts get mullioned glass, a fascia signboard lettered from the
  district's own cast (`kit/signage.js` atlas, Space Grotesk), scalloped or fixed awnings, hanging
  signs, spotlights and window displays. Roofs: flat with parapet + water tank/AC/aerials/stair
  hut, pitched with chimney stacks, mansard with dormers, sawtooth with glazing, pagoda tiers,
  veranda with posts and railings. Archetypes are chosen from keywords in each zone's authored
  `architecture` text (georgian / brick / shop / tower / industrial / veranda / adobe / pagoda /
  mansard / painted) and every trim colour derives from the zone palette. Bright-painted
  districts (Jellybean Cove, Bo-Kaap, Carnival Avenue) get per-house colours; brick districts
  with walk-ups get fire escapes; festive ones get bunting and string lights.
- **Streets have a language.** Paver fields with per-slab tint and kerb-side wear, asphalt with
  lane wear, chamfered 12 cm kerb stones along every carriageway with dropped kerbs and tactile
  blister paving at every corner, zebra at the stop, manholes, bollards, slatted benches, lamps
  with lantern heads and soft light cones, bins, hydrants (US/CA), tree pits with grates, planters
  with hedges and flowers, café terraces with umbrellas and chairs, a glass bus shelter, a bike
  rack, street-name plates, and the national post box and phone box by country prefix (UK pillar
  box + K6, Irish green pillar, US blue box, Canadian red, Caribbean post-mounted).
- **Vegetation has species.** `kit/flora.js`: multi-lobe oaks/planes (normals re-pointed from the
  canopy centre for soft toon shading), pines, palms with 8-9 curved ribbon fronds and coconuts,
  jacarandas, gums, maples, live oaks with Spanish moss, tree ferns, rowans with berries. Species
  mix per district comes from climate keywords and the country prefix; boulevard trees take the
  species of the district they stand beside; 3000 wind-swayed grass tufts cover the parkland.
- **Every district has a landmark vista.** `kit/landmarks.js` picks one of 16 kinds from the
  zone's `landmark` line (arch, footbridge, clock tower, lighthouse, bandstand, statue, windmill,
  pier with ferris wheel, carved meeting house / pagoda, round tower, great tree, gantry cranes,
  diner, obelisk/cross, fountain) and places it on a paved apron at the far end of the centre
  street, so the arrival view down the district terminates on it.
- **The hub is a forecourt.** Concentric paving rings, a terracotta rim and a real kerb, four
  colonnade segments with a station clock, two kit fountains where the flat pools were, twelve
  kit palms in planters, slatted benches, planters, bins, bike racks, flagpoles, manholes. The
  bench that sat across the spawn's first step is gone: the ring now leaves the spawn radial and
  the radial toward Conductor Clara open.
- **Boulevards** are batched per line: asphalt with lane wear, paver sidewalks, chamfered kerbs in
  the line colour, dashes, arrows, zebra + stop bar, manholes; track bed, rails, sleepers,
  catenary, classic lamps with cones, platform, glass canopy, buffer stop, emergency cabinet,
  signals. Trams are 4 meshes each (was 7), traffic is 5 instanced fleets with a role-attribute
  shader (was 30 meshes / 6 materials). No material anywhere in my files has metalness > 0.2;
  chrome is a light toon tone.
- **Streaming feels smooth.** Dispose-before-build; one chunk build in flight, run as a generator
  under an 8 ms per-tick budget (ground, then one building per step, furniture, merge+AO, people);
  a zone the player is already inside (metro arrival) builds synchronously behind the travel
  fade; chunks fade in over 0.6 s; fine dressing is only drawn within 46 m and street/roof
  furniture within 80 m; the first chunk pre-warms every district shader once.
- **Locals no longer pop.** Bodies cross-fade over 44-50 m (opacity 1.0 / 0.67 / 0.17 / 0 measured
  at 40 / 46 / 49 / 52 m); a body only casts a shadow within 22 m. Hats sit on the measured head
  (was +0.12 m floating). Vehicles and trams push the player out of their footprint.
- Conductor Clara's role is **Station Conductor**.

## Known gaps / handoff

- `crowd.speakers` still grows by ~21 per stream cycle (69 → 330 over 12 cycles in
  `city-verify.json`): that is the game lane's `crowd.js despawn()` hunk (runtime-crowd-speakers-
  never-removed); my `disposeChunk` despawns correctly, the speaker list is theirs.
- zones.json roles "kapa haka tutor" / "steelpan tutor" (content-06) are gamedata (game lane).
- The shadow pass is ~45% of all triangles at every view; 245k of it is skinned bodies (hero +
  locals) and 150k is hub GLB props reached by the shadow camera's depth along the light
  direction. Both are outside this lane's files; a 2-cascade or light-space-fitted frustum
  (render lane) and decimated locals (character lane) would recover most of it.
- SwiftShader on this shared VPS builds a chunk in 100-220 ms of JS; the generator spreads it
  over ticks, but the numbers in `lastBuildProfile` are not what a laptop will see.
